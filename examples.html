English in real life

Useful words for everyday moments.

Read the short dialogues. Then try to use one sentence in your own life.

01

Introducing Yourself

Anna: Hi, my name is Anna. What's your name?

Dara: I'm Dara. Nice to meet you.

Anna: Nice to meet you, too. Where are you from?

Dara: I'm from Cambodia.

ខ្មែរ: សួស្តី ខ្ញុំឈ្មោះអាណា។ អ្នកឈ្មោះអ្វី? រីករាយដែលបានស្គាល់អ្នក។

Vocabularyname - ឈ្មោះfrom - មកពីmeet - ជួប
02

At School

Teacher: Please open your books.

Student: Which page, please?

Teacher: Page twenty-four. Read the first paragraph.

Student: Yes, teacher.

ខ្មែរ: សូមបើកសៀវភៅ។ ទំព័រណា? ទំព័រទី២៤។

Vocabularypage - ទំព័រread - អានbook - សៀវភៅ
03

At a Restaurant

Waiter: Are you ready to order?

Customer: Yes. I'd like chicken and rice, please.

Waiter: Would you like a drink?

Customer: Some water, please.

ខ្មែរ: តើអ្នករួចរាល់ក្នុងការកុម្ម៉ង់ឬនៅ? ខ្ញុំចង់បានមាន់ និងបាយ។

Vocabularyorder - កុម្ម៉ង់drink - ភេសជ្ជៈwater - ទឹក
04

Shopping

Customer: How much is this shirt?

Shop assistant: It is fifteen dollars.

Customer: Do you have it in blue?

Shop assistant: Yes, here you are.

ខ្មែរ: អាវនេះថ្លៃប៉ុន្មាន? តើមានពណ៌ខៀវទេ?

Vocabularyshirt - អាវprice - តម្លៃblue - ពណ៌ខៀវ
05

Asking for Directions

Tourist: Excuse me. Where is the bank?

Local: Go straight and turn left.

Tourist: Is it near the market?

Local: Yes, it is next to the pharmacy.

ខ្មែរ: សូមទោស តើធនាគារនៅឯណា? ទៅត្រង់ ហើយបត់ឆ្វេង។

Vocabularystraight - ត្រង់turn - បត់next to - ជាប់នឹង
06

Daily Routine

Sam: What time do you get up?

Lin: I get up at six thirty.

Sam: What do you do next?

Lin: I have breakfast and go to work.

ខ្មែរ: តើអ្នកក្រោកម៉ោងប៉ុន្មាន? ខ្ញុំក្រោកម៉ោងប្រាំមួយកន្លះ។

Vocabularyget up - ក្រោកពីគេងbreakfast - អាហារពេលព្រឹកnext - បន្ទាប់
07

Talking About Hobbies

Kim: What do you do in your free time?

Vannak: I like playing football.

Kim: How often do you play?

Vannak: I play every Saturday.

ខ្មែរ: តើអ្នកធ្វើអ្វីនៅពេលទំនេរ? ខ្ញុំចូលចិត្តលេងបាល់ទាត់។

Vocabularyhobby - ចំណង់ចំណូលចិត្តfree time - ពេលទំនេរoften - ញឹកញាប់
