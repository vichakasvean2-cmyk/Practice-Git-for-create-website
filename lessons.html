01 / GRAMMAR

Present Simple

Use it for habits, routines, and facts.

Simple explanation

Use the present simple for things that happen often or are generally true.

Grammar rule

I / you / we / they + verb
he / she / it + verb + s

I study English every day.

She works at a school.

Do you like coffee?

He does not play tennis.

Try it

Complete the sentence:

My brother ____ (watch) TV at night.

02 / GRAMMAR

Present Continuous

Use it for something happening now.

Simple explanation

Use am / is / are + verb-ing for an action happening at this moment.

Examples

I am reading a book.

She is talking to her friend.

They are playing football.

Are you listening?

Try it

Complete the sentence:

Look! The baby ____ (sleep).

ខ្មែរ: មើល! ទារកកំពុងដេក។

03 / GRAMMAR

Past Simple

Talk about finished actions in the past.

Grammar rule

Use a past verb. Regular verbs usually end in -ed. Some verbs are irregular.

Examples

I visited my aunt yesterday.

We watched a film last night.

She went to the market.

Did you enjoy the trip?

Try it

Complete the sentence:

They ____ (play) tennis yesterday.

04 / GRAMMAR

Future with “going to”

Talk about plans for the future.

Grammar rule

am / is / are + going to + verb

Examples

We are going to visit Phnom Penh.

05 / GRAMMAR

Countable and Uncountable Nouns

Know when you can count something.

Simple explanation

Countable nouns can have a number: one apple, two apples. Uncountable nouns cannot: water, rice, money.

Examples

There are three books.

We need some rice.

I have an idea.

Try it

Is “milk” countable?

Milk is a(n) ____ noun.

ខ្មែរ: ទឹកដោះគោគឺជានាមមិនអាចរាប់បាន។

06 / GRAMMAR

Some and Any

Use these words with plural and uncountable nouns.

Grammar rule

Use some in positive sentences. Use any in questions and negative sentences.

Examples

There are some eggs.

Do you have any sugar?

There is not any cheese.

Try it

Complete the sentence:

Are there ____ apples?

07 / GRAMMAR

Much and Many

Talk about quantity.

Grammar rule

Use many with countable nouns and much with uncountable nouns.

Examples

How many students are there?

How much water do you drink?

There are not many chairs.

Try it

Complete the sentence:

How ____ money do you need?

08 / COMMUNICATION

Can and Can't

Talk about ability and permission.

Grammar rule

Use can + verb for ability. Use can't + verb for something you cannot do.

Examples

I can swim.

She can speak English.

We can't come today.

Try it

Complete the sentence:

Can you ____ (help) me?

ខ្មែរ: តើអ្នកអាចជួយខ្ញុំបានទេ?

09 / GRAMMAR

Comparative Adjectives

Compare two people or things.

Grammar rule

For short adjectives, add -er + than. For long adjectives, use more + adjective.

Examples

My bag is bigger than yours.

A car is faster than a bicycle.

This book is more interesting.

Try it

Complete the sentence:

My sister is ____ (tall) than me.

10 / GRAMMAR

Prepositions of Place

Say where something is.

Grammar rule

in means inside, on means on a surface, and under means below something.

Examples

The keys are on the table.

The cat is under the chair.

She is in the classroom.

Try it

Complete the sentence:

The book is ____ the bag.

ខ្មែរ: សៀវភៅនៅក្នុងកាបូប។

11 / VOCABULARY

Everyday Vocabulary

Useful words for your daily life.

Words to remember

morning - the first part of the day
schedule - a plan of times
neighbour - a person who lives near you
borrow - take and return something

Examples

I check my schedule every morning.

My neighbour is very friendly.

Can I borrow your pen?

Try it

Choose the correct word:

Can I ____ your book?
